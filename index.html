Frequently Asked Questions

What are Bingwa Sokoni Data Bundles?

Bingwa Sokoni data bundles are affordable internet, minute, and SMS packages offered through Safaricom's Bingwa Sokoni platform. They are designed for customers looking for flexible, daily, weekly, or monthly mobile data and communication options.

How do I buy Bingwa Sokoni bundles?

You can buy bundles by paying the exact offer price via M-Pesa to the Till Number 5797885 (Captain Charles Data Solution). Follow the “How to Buy” steps above to complete your purchase.

How much do Bingwa Sokoni data bundles cost?

Prices start from as low as KSh 22 for a 1GB 1-hour data bundle. Offers range up to KSh 1,000 for monthly bundles with large data, minutes, and SMS allocations. Check the offer cards above for full pricing.

Which Bingwa Sokoni offers can I buy once per day?

The “Data Bundles” category (1GB, 250MB, 750MB+50SMS, 400MB, 1.5GB, 6GB) can be purchased only once per number per day. These are marked with the “1 offer per day” badge.

Can I buy Tunukiwa offers more than once?

Yes! Tunukiwa offers (1GB for KSh 23 and 2GB for KSh 105) can be purchased multiple times per day.

Can I buy Bingwa Sokoni bundles if I have Okoa Jahazi debt?

Yes. You can purchase eligible Bingwa Sokoni offers even if you have an outstanding Safaricom Okoa Jahazi debt. Simply choose the offer you want and complete payment using the M-Pesa instructions provided on this page. Your Okoa Jahazi debt remains unchanged — you're simply able to buy Bingwa Sokoni data with Okoa Jahazi without the debt blocking your purchase.

How do I pay for Bingwa Sokoni bundles?

Payment is made via M-Pesa using the “Lipa Na M-Pesa” > “Buy Goods & Services” option. Enter Till Number 5797885 (Captain Charles Data Solution) and the exact amount of the bundle you want.

What is the M-Pesa Till Number?

The Till Number for purchasing Bingwa Sokoni bundles is 5797885 (Captain Charles Data Solution). Use this number when making your M-Pesa payment.

Are there Bingwa Sokoni minutes and SMS offers?

Yes. We offer all-net minutes bundles starting from KSh 21 for 45 minutes, and SMS bundles starting from KSh 5 for 20 SMS. See the “All-Net Minutes” and “SMS Bundles” sections above.

Which Bingwa Sokoni bundles are valid for 30 days?

The monthly data bundles (21GB, 1.2GB, 2.5GB, and 8GB+400 Minutes+1000 SMS) are valid for 30 days. The 1,250 minutes and 1,500 SMS bundles are also valid for 30 days.
